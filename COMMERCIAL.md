# Commercial use

Windkeel is **source-available**, not open source. It is licensed under the
[PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE).

## Free

You can use, run, change and share Windkeel for free for any **noncommercial** purpose, including:

- Personal projects, hobby projects, study and research
- Evaluating Windkeel to decide whether to buy it
- Charities, schools, public research organizations and government institutions, as the license
  defines them

## Commercial use needs a paid plan or a license

If you use Windkeel for a commercial purpose, for example to verify pull requests in your
company's repositories or CI, or to offer it as part of a product or service, you need one of:

1. **A paid plan on the managed service.** The managed Windkeel factory includes the gate.
   See [pricing](https://console-two-gamma.vercel.app/pricing).
2. **A commercial license** to run the CLI, the GitHub Action or the factory yourself.
   [Ask for one](https://github.com/jyothepro/windkeel/issues/new?template=access.yml) and say
   it is for a commercial license; we will follow up privately.

If you are not sure whether your use is commercial, ask. We would rather say yes than have you
guess.
